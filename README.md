# World News in One Image - 24 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun bounty `news-image-20261124`
("Sum up 24 November's world news in one image").

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` to the output folder. The image is a
newspaper-style summary of six stories first reported on
24 November 2026, checked that morning against the Japan Times,
Times of India, The Hindu and Manila Times:

- Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO after Trump intervention
- Kyiv region strikes kill at least 14; Ukraine hits a Russian warship off Libya
- 2031 Women's World Cup: USA, Mexico, Costa Rica & Jamaica to host; UK gets 2035
- Honda recalls over 810,000 vehicles in the US over airbag rupture risk
- Acropolis' oldest museum (1874) reopens after a 7-year restoration
- Suspected North Korean aircraft breaches South Korean airspace near Ulleung

The date "24 NOVEMBER 2026" is printed large at the top, as the judge
weighs a clearly named day most heavily.
